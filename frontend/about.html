Temple History

Shree Shanishwara Swamy Temple is a sacred place of worship dedicated to Lord Shanishwara Swamy. The temple is located at Shri Kshetra Siddagiri in Kusugundi Village, Koduru Post, Hosanagara Taluk, Shivamogga District, Karnataka. It is surrounded by a peaceful natural environment that creates a spiritual atmosphere for devotees. The greenery around the temple adds to the calmness and beauty of the temple premises. The temple provides devotees with a place to pray, worship and spend time in spiritual reflection. Devotees visit the temple with faith and devotion towards Lord Shanishwara Swamy.

Importance of Lord Shanishwara

Lord Shanishwara is an important deity in Hindu tradition and is also commonly known as Shani Dev. Lord Shanishwara is traditionally associated with the planet Saturn and is one of the Navagrahas in Hindu tradition. The worship of Lord Shanishwara has an important place in the spiritual practices of many devotees. Devotees approach Lord Shanishwara with faith, respect and devotion.

Lord Shanishwara is traditionally associated with justice and responsibility and is also connected with the results of a person's actions. The concept of karma is closely associated with the traditional understanding of Shani, reminding devotees that actions have consequences and that people should live responsibly. Worship of Lord Shanishwara encourages devotees to reflect on their actions and reminds them of the importance of honesty and discipline.
