Welcome to the Temple

Shree Shanishwara Swamy Temple, nestled at the sacred Siddagiri hill in Kusugundi village, is a revered shrine dedicated to Lord Shanishwara. The temple holds immense spiritual significance and has been a beacon of faith for generations.
